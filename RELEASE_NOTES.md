Punchy 0.1.7 brings the complete implementation of **Punchy! Moves** alongside an overhaul of mod settings and a live in-game HUD editor:

- **Interactive In-Game Live HUD Editor (F9)**: press **F9** while playing in any world to display an on-screen translucent overlay. Switch sections with `Tab`, select parameters with `↑`/`↓`, and adjust values live with `←`/`→` or `-`/`=` to watch your character, camera, and arms shift in real time!
- **Overhauled Settings Screen**: under **Options > Mods > Punchy Rust > Settings**, every parameter is displayed with dedicated `[-]` and `[+]` stepping buttons and exact numerical feedback instead of blind menu cycling.
- **Third-person shoulder camera**: over-the-shoulder perspective with smooth multi-sample collision raycasting against terrain and obstacles.
- **Free / orbit camera (F8)**: inspect your character and surroundings freely; moving the mouse rotates the camera angle around the player without twisting character aim.
- **Stationary turn-in-place easing**: realistic body yaw rotation curve with a 50-degree head-body differential constraint.
- **First-person viewmodel adjustments**: retains full arm and item position/scale customization from 0.1.6.

Requires Horizon Nightly 2026-11-05 (`66f3981`), Minecraft 26.3. Install the `.hnmod` bundle and rebuild the native game in the launcher.
